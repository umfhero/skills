# Provenance

Anti-slop was written following static reviews of three user-supplied skill snapshots and the author's installed stop-slopv4. Its implementation and synthetic evaluation examples were written for this package; upstream detector code, service integrations, publishing workflows and private author samples were not imported.

- **stop-slop**, Hardik Pandya, supplied `stop-slop-main` snapshot: informed the editorial pattern categories and modular references. Its absolute bans and numerical self-score were rejected.
- **[Avoid AI Writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing)**, Conor Bronsdon, supplied version 3.34.0: informed protected content, separation of editing and verification, explicit evidence status and bounded repair. The supplied notice attributes ChatGPT/Codex orchestration contributions to Mamdouh Aboammar. Its detector classifications and semantic-preservation claims were not adopted.
- **[HumanizerAI agent skills](https://github.com/humanizerai/agent-skills)**, HumanizerAI, supplied snapshot: informed audience/purpose intake and separate diagnostics. No API, credit workflow or detector-bypass claim was adopted.
- **stop-slopv4**, the author's existing skill: informed British English, connected prose and register distinctions. Contradictory bans, invented specifics in examples and sentence-length quotas were replaced.

These are acknowledgements of reviewed material, not endorsements or runtime dependencies. The supplied MIT notices are retained in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The audit reports and source hashes remain outside this installable package; the downloaded example folders can be removed without breaking the skill.
